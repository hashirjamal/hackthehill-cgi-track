WHAT CHANGED FROM OUR ORIGINAL PLAN
===================================

Our first assumption was wrong
------------------------------
We assumed a separate data team prepared and organised the complaint data before
staff worked on it. There isn't one. The same people who resolve complaints also
have to dig through Northwind's systems and piece the information together
themselves.

Today, one complaint means visiting every system by hand
--------------------------------------------------------
Each system holds a different piece of the picture:

  - Helix has the account summary and customer context for 1.8 million accounts.
  - Aurora Billing has only the billing information.
  - The other systems hold call notes, case history and customer messages.

Staff go into each system one by one, copy what they need, and re-enter it into
their main platform before they can even start fixing the problem.

Our new approach removes that manual search
-------------------------------------------
1. One simple intake form.
   When a complaint comes in by phone or email, the staff member records only
   what they have at that moment: what the customer said, in plain language,
   plus their account number. No searching, no system hopping.

2. Laya classifies it instantly.
   Laya reads the plain-language text and sorts the complaint into the right
   category and urgency level.

3. The right team sees it, ranked.
   Each team (for example Billing) opens the dashboard, filters to its own
   section, and sees its complaints ranked by urgency, so the most urgent cases
   are handled first.

4. An AI agent gathers the information.
   Instead of a person searching every system, a local AI agent has tools that
   connect to each one: Helix, Aurora Billing, the call centre system, the case
   system and the customer portal. It pulls the relevant data, makes sense of it,
   and gives the staff member clear next steps and a draft reply. For the demo
   we simulate these systems with realistic data.

The result
----------
Staff spend their time fixing the complaint, not hunting for information across
five systems.
